Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what you can do about it. No legalese where we can avoid it.

Last updated 30 September 2026About a 2 minute read
On this page
What we collect How we use it Google sign-in Where it's stored Your choices Contact

01What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, either typed when you sign up or provided by Google if you use "Continue with Google". Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Your content: the star ratings and optional written reviews you submit. Reviews are public and shown next to your name.
We don't collect payment details, location or contacts, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

02How we use it

Only to run the site: signing you in, saving your ratings, and showing averages and reviews. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

03Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

04Where it's stored

Account and rating data are stored with our database provider, Supabase. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Small preferences such as theme, layout and saved models stay in your own browser and never reach our servers.

05Your choices

  • Edit or delete any of your ratings on the site at any time.
  • Clear saved models and preferences by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • To delete your account and everything linked to it, open an issue and we'll remove it.

06Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub.
