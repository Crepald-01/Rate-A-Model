Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, either entered when you sign up or provided by Google if you use "Continue with Google". If you sign up with a password, we never see it in plain text.
  • Your content: the star ratings and optional written reviews you submit, shown publicly next to your name.

We do not collect payment details, location, or contacts, and we don't run advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

Only to run the site: signing you in, saving your ratings, and showing averages and reviews. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it's stored

Account and rating data are stored with our provider, Supabase. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any of your ratings on the site at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, open an issue at github.com/Crepald-01/Rate-A-Model/issues and we will remove it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
