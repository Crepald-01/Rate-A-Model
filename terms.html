01Using the site

Rate the Models is a free community site for rating AI models. By creating an account or using the site you agree to these terms.

02Your account

  • Keep your login details safe. You're responsible for activity under your account.
  • Use a real email address.
  • Don't impersonate other people.

03Your content

Ratings and reviews you post are public and shown with your name. Post only your own honest opinions.

  • No spam, hate, or harassment.
  • No personal information about other people.
  • Nothing illegal.
We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

04No guarantees

Ratings are personal opinions from users, not professional advice or verified benchmarks. Model information is provided as-is and may be incomplete or out of date. The site is provided without warranties and may change or stop at any time.
